**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: free for personal and commercial projects, no credit required, given here anyway.

- Pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (the pack's own `License.txt` says the same)

The files are the pack's own `.ogg` files, copied unchanged (not edited, not re-encoded, same names):

| File here | File in the pack | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `cloth1.ogg`, `cloth2.ogg`, `cloth3.ogg`, `cloth4.ogg` | `Audio/cloth1.ogg` to `Audio/cloth4.ogg` | the brush of step 6: while the brush is down, `brush.gd` plays the four in turn with 0.12 s of quiet between them, its volume following the speed of the head |
